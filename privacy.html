Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Naharak Salata ("we") runs a private tool that publishes short animated videos made by us to our own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the tool handles.

What we collect

  • Our own account tokens. When we connect our own TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account, the platform gives us access tokens. We store them securely on our own computer and use them only to publish our videos and read basic information about our own account (for example the account name).
  • No data from other users. The tool does not log in other people, and it does not collect, store or sell personal data of viewers or followers.

How we use it

Tokens are used only to upload and publish our own videos and to check the publishing status. They are never shared with anyone or used for advertising.

Third-party services

Publishing goes through the official APIs of TikTok, Meta (Instagram) and Google (YouTube); their own privacy policies apply on their platforms.

Deleting data

We can disconnect the tool at any time; stored tokens are then deleted. You can also revoke access from the platform settings.

Children

Our content is family friendly. The tool does not collect any information from children or anyone else.

Contact

Questions: send us a message on Instagram or TikTok.
